# B Tech Ed IT & BIT (Dual Degree)

-----------------
About My Degree
-----------------

The Bachelor of Technical Education in Information Technology and BIT is a five-year double degree program offered by Kathmandu University, combining the four-year Bachelor of Technical Education in Information Technology (B. Tech Ed IT) with the four-year Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT).  This collaborative initiative, formalized via a Memorandum of Understanding between the School of Education and the School of Engineering, allows students enrolled in the B. Tech Ed IT program to pursue the BIT component after completing their initial degree. 

The program structure involves 136 credit hours for the B. Tech Ed IT and an additional 30 credit hours for the BIT, totaling 166 credit hours over 4.5 years.

Graduates gain competencies in both technical-vocational education and information technology, enabling careers in educational technology, industry, and further postgraduate studies.
